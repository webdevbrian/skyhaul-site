The short version

Rocket Haul doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game doesn't connect to the internet.

What the game stores

Your progress (cash, upgrades, stats and challenges) and your settings are saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device, except as part of your device's own backups if you use them. Deleting the app deletes this data, and Settings → Reset Progress in the game wipes your progress at any time.

Motion sensors

If you steer by tilting your phone, the game reads your device's orientation while you play, only to steer the rocket. Nothing about it is recorded or sent anywhere. You can switch to touch steering in the game's settings.

Information we collect

None. Rocket Haul doesn't collect, sell or share personal information, and it doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising code. When you buy Rocket Haul, the App Store or Google Play handles the purchase; we never see your name or payment details.

Children

Rocket Haul collects no information from anyone, children included.
