How do I play?

Up: tilt your phone to steer and dodge everything. Hold the screen to boost. The first thing you hit (or running out of fuel) stalls the rocket.
Down: steer into things to tractor-beam them. Hold the screen to open your parachute and fall slower.
Blast: your haul flies into the air. Tap to shoot it for cash, and don't shoot the jellies. They cost you.

I'd rather not tilt my phone

Tap the gear on the home screen and switch Steering to Touch, then drag a finger to steer. You can also change how sensitive tilt steering is there.

There's no sound

Rocket Haul follows your iPhone's silent switch, so turn the ringer on. Also check Sound and Music in the game's settings (the gear on the home screen).

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button in the top-left corner during a run. The game also pauses by itself if you leave the app.

How do I start over?

Gear → Reset Progress. This wipes your cash, upgrades, areas, stats and challenges, and can't be undone. Your settings stay.
